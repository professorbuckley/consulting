Audit Services

One audit. Scoped to what you actually need.

I provide independent expert audits of brands and digital experiences. The engagement is scoped around the areas that need attention, rather than forcing every organization into a predefined package. That might mean a focused review of one area or a broader assessment across several. The point is to understand what is working, what is not, and what deserves attention before committing to more production.

Area 01

Brand

Visual identity, typography, color, imagery, messaging, consistency, and how the brand is expressed across key touchpoints.

Area 02

Website + Digital Experience

Navigation, information architecture, content hierarchy, responsiveness, accessibility, performance, and the overall digital experience.

Area 03

UX/UI

Interface consistency, interaction patterns, calls to action, usability friction, hierarchy, discoverability, and common UX issues.

Area 04

AI Content + Experience

AI-assisted content, imagery, interfaces, and customer-facing experiences, including generic language, factual or contextual problems, brand drift, inconsistency, unnecessary automation, and the places where human judgment has quietly left the room.

In less formal terms: an AI slop audit.
What You Get

Not another 100-page strategy deck.

The goal is a concise, usable assessment and a prioritized roadmap. Findings distinguish between contained improvements that can be addressed now and systemic issues that require broader intervention.

What's working

Elements worth preserving rather than changing simply for the sake of change.

What needs attention now

Contained issues that can often be corrected within the existing brand or experience.

What requires deeper intervention

Structural issues involving branding, information architecture, content, UX, technology, or the way AI is being used across the experience.

What happens next

Prioritized recommendations and guidance on what can be handled internally and where specialist support may be useful.

Why It Matters

Design problems have consequences.

Poor hierarchy creates confusion. Inconsistent interfaces create friction. Accessibility problems exclude users. Slow experiences contribute to abandonment. Weak messaging makes it harder for people to understand what an organization offers and what they should do next.

Approach

From observation to action.

The audit is designed to move beyond a list of design opinions. Good judgment requires more than spotting what looks wrong. Each significant finding connects an issue to its impact, a recommendation, an intended outcome, and the likely scope of implementation.

01

Assess

02

Identify

03

04
