Ethical Design Consulting

Technologies shape society and human behavior. That influence raises a question about what ends digital products and services should serve. Ethical reasoning and a teleological view (one that treats human flourishing as an end worth pursuing) can guide what we build and how we build it.

I offer ethical assessments of digital products and services, recommendations for improvement, and workshops on ethical design.

Drawing on my experience as a design practitioner and scholar, I use concepts from normative ethics and political philosophy to evaluate design decisions and ground my recommendations in virtue ethics, with particular attention to practical wisdom (phronēsis), justice, courage, honesty, and temperance to guide recommendations.

If you want to build useful digital products and services that also help people live well, let’s talk.
